   ## End of Session

   1. Show the audience's **scoreboard** on the left.
   2. Show the change in breakfast rating scores (rating 2 - rating 1) on the right with comments:
      - If rating 2 > rating 1, then "Great - your rating has increased. This may show that you see more value in breakfast after the session."
      - If rating 2 < rating 1, then "That’s okay - your rating has decreased. The session may have helped you think more critically about whether breakfast fits your own routine."
      - If rating 2 = rating 1 < 5, then "Your rating has stayed the same. Breakfast may still not feel like a priority, but you now have some practical options if you choose to try it."
      - If ratomg 2 = rating 1 >= 5, then "Your rating has stayed the same. You already saw breakfast as important, and this session may help you make your choices more balanced and practical."
   3. Below the scoreboard and the breakfast rating change, show "Reminder: do not forget to complete your activites and upload your photos [here](link to the \"Forum\" page)."

   If the audience click the link to the forum, open the forum page in the same tab in the browser.

   ## Forum page behaviour

   - Show posts that audience has uploaded on the page:
      - if the post has more than one photos, show the first main photo
      - if the post has a very long passage, show only the first 100 words with ...
      - allow audience to click "like" (heart symbol)
   -  When audience clicks a post, move to the post's link:
       - within a post, allow audience to click "like" (heart symbol)
      - within a post, allow audience to leave messages within the post's link, but do not show the comments on the "Forum" page
      - withink a post, show the number of "like"s
   - At the bottom of the "Forum" page, leave space for the audience to add a post with name, comments and photos:
      - once the post is added, create an individual link for the post
   - Owner can delete any post/comment via an admin-only delete button (gated by a password or owner email login)
